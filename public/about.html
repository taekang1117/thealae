About The Alae

Tae Jong Kang

Independent tennis analysis with a clear line between reported facts, model output, and personal opinion.

The Alae is written and published by Tae Jong Kang. It focuses on ATP and Grand Slam matchups: serve patterns, rally shape, form, and the ways a model can help frame—but not replace—a tennis judgment.

What readers can expect

Every analysis separates verifiable match facts from The Alae’s own interpretation. When a piece uses rankings, draws, results, or player information, it links to the relevant primary or official source where practical. Charts and model estimates are labeled as The Alae’s work.

Editorial approach

The site is independent and does not present opinions as guarantees. A published pick is a time-stamped view based on the information available at the time. Material corrections are made in the article itself and reflected in its update time.
